Both Aurora cores are part of the hardware.

One generator sends payload through Aurora 0 to Aurora 1. A local golden-reference FIFO records the transmitted words for comparison. Aurora 1's application TX is idle; the reverse serial path still carries Aurora link traffic.

Why RX is called an AXI masterAurora supplies received data on USER_DATA_M_AXIS_RX. Master describes its role on the local AXI stream; RX describes the direction on the serial link.
Hardware and simulation share the same designThe testbench supplies two clock pairs, a reset pulse and a cable model. Clocking Wizard, startup reset logic, both Aurora cores and ILA remain inside the hardware wrapper.
